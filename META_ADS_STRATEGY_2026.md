## Why Meta Ads for AzadiWish?

### ✅ The Viral Loop Model

```
Meta Ads (Paid)
    ↓
10K seed users
    ↓
30% share rate = 3K WhatsApp shares
    ↓
40% recipient conversion = 1.2K new creators
    ↓
These 1.2K share again (organic loop)
    ↓
Exponential viral growth Aug 10–15
    ↓
50K–200K total uniques (mostly organic)
    ↓
$500–2K ad spend → $5K–15K revenue
```

### Why Aug 6–9?

| Period | Strategy | Why |
|--------|----------|-----|
| **Aug 6–9** | Run Meta Ads | Seed viral loop before peak |
| **Aug 10–15** | Stop ads, let viral spread | Organic traffic = highest quality |
| **Aug 15** | Peak traffic day | 80% from WhatsApp shares |
| **Aug 16+** | Tail traffic | Archive + email reminders |

### ✅ Monetization Advantage

Running Meta Ads Aug 6–9:
- **Paid traffic** → Use Monetag (AdSense-safe, no ban risk)
- Monetag CPM: $10–15 (better than Media.net $3–8)
- **Organic traffic** Aug 10–15 → Switch to AdSense later
- AdSense CPM: $5–10

**Total blended revenue:** Higher than organic-only

## Tracking & Optimization

### Key Metrics to Monitor

**In Meta Ads Manager:**
- **CPC (Cost Per Click):** Target < $0.40
- **CTR (Click-Through Rate):** Target > 1.5%
- **CPM (Cost Per Mille):** Monitor for efficiency
- **Frequency:** Keep < 3 (avoid ad fatigue)
- **ROAS:** Not directly applicable (we optimize for reach, not direct sales)

**In Google Analytics 4:**
- **Session source:** utm_source=facebook
- **Session medium:** utm_medium=cpc
- **Share rate:** share_click / sessions
- **Viral coefficient:** new_creators / previous_creators

**In Monetag Dashboard:**
- **Daily impressions:** Should spike Aug 6–9
- **Fill rate:** Target 90%+
- **Earnings by zone:** Track top performers

### Optimization Rules

**If CPC > $0.50:**
- Pause underperforming adsets
- Scale budget from high-performing adsets
- Try different creatives (test new images/videos)

**If CTR < 1%:**
- Change creative (use video instead of static)
- Adjust ad copy (shorter, punchier)
- Target different audience (try Tier 2/3 instead of Tier 1)

**If fill rate < 80%:**
- Switch Monetag zones to OnClick format (100% fill)
- Reduce ad slots (focus quality over quantity)

**If viral coefficient < 1:**
- Ads aren't seeding the loop
- Increase ad spend to 10K+ seed users
- Optimize share flow (Web Share API is working?)

## FAQ

### Q: Do I need Meta Pixel for ads?
**A:** No, but HIGHLY recommended. Pixel lets you:
- Track clicks and conversions
- Build retargeting audiences
- Create lookalike audiences
- Measure campaign ROI

### Q: Can I run ads without Monetag ads on site?
**A:** Yes, but earnings will be lower:
- AdSense can show ads on paid traffic BUT with risk
- Monetag is risk-free for paid traffic
- Recommend using Monetag Aug 6–9, switch to AdSense Aug 9+

### Q: What if ads don't get approved?
**A:** Meta usually approves Independence Day ads instantly because:
- Not political (celebrating national holiday)
- Not promoting products
- No restricted categories

If rejected, add: "This is a free utility app celebrating Indian Independence Day. No commercial intent."

### Q: Can I run ads in other languages?
**A:** Yes! For Tier 2/3:
- Use Hindi creatives
- Target Hindi-speaking audiences
- Use regional interest targeting

### Q: What's the best time to start ads?
**A:** Aug 6 (10 days before peak). Any earlier = wasted spend on low search intent.

### Q: Should I run ads after Aug 9?
**A:** No. Aug 10–15 is peak viral period. Organic traffic > paid traffic. Save budget for next year's festivals (Diwali, Republic Day, etc.).
